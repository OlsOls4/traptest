TrapTest — provider leaderboard

Builders choosing a transaction-intelligence provider on Telegraph need to know whose answers can be trusted before they spend — this page buys the same twelve trap queries, whose correct answers are known in advance, from five competing providers and scores each answer pass or fail, with receipts. It matters because a wrong answer sold as intelligence costs buyers money and trust, and three of the five providers could not serve a single trap.

Honest limits

Testnet use is not adoption, and no user, retention, accuracy, or time-saved figure is claimed. The entry is single-Intent (ONCHAIN_TX_LOOKUP_GENERAL). n=12 traps per provider is small: a two-trap margin (Truvian 4/12 vs TxLens 2/12) is evidence, not a proven gap. Schema gaps are not lies: Truvian returns no status for confirmed transactions (its block/from/to/value were all correct on 6 such traps) — those traps score FAIL under the strict rules and the gap is reported as a gap, not as a wrong answer. TxLens's not_found answers ARE wrong answers (the txs are mined and confirmed), not a schema gap. Failed calls can still settle: Sigil and OnChain Intel failed every call, yet $0.01 per call settled on-chain into Telegraph escrow credit (not billed).
